The history

The art

I’m a designer and engineer from Lagos, Lagos State. I studied Computer Science at the Federal University of Petroleum Resources, Effurun, and that grounding in algorithms, data structures and networking still shapes how I design: I like to know exactly how something will be built before I draw it.

Before working independently I worked inside agencies, including Branditechture, building websites and taking them live end to end. Alongside that I took on remote freelance work for small and medium-sized businesses that needed a real digital foundation, not just a logo.

The way

Deao Studios is built around simplified communication. Launching a business online shouldn’t mean juggling a designer, a developer and a hosting company who never talk to each other. Here the brand, the interface, the website and the server come from one person who understands all of them.

A useful brand is one a business can actually run. I work with African SMEs competing in a digital-first market, and I set projects up with clear guidelines and properly configured infrastructure, so you stay in control after launch.
